19th ISTE Convention

PESCE Campus • Completed

Overview

The 19th ISTE Karnataka State-Level Student Convention, held on June 7, 2024, at PESCE Mandya, gathered 600+ students, educators, and leaders from over 200 institutions—a landmark gathering for innovation, entrepreneurship, and technical excellence.

Themed “Empowering Young Minds for Innovative Startup Culture,” the event featured a grand opening with keynotes from industry and ISTE national leaders, and a vibrant cultural program.

25+ technical and non-technical events were hosted: hackathons, coding battles, robotics, quizzes, poster-making, debates, and workshops, challenging participants in every domain and showcasing creative thinking.

The organizing committee, comprising faculty and a large student core, managed months of meticulous planning, seamless registration, logistics, and guest coordination for this large-scale convention.

The event promoted cross-campus networking, career mentorship, new technology exploration, and peer-to-peer collaboration. Attendees gained new technical skills, blockchain-certified credentials, and lasting bonds.

Standout moments included the talent-spotlight award ceremony, Best Student Chapter honors, and a closing that left participants motivated for future technological achievement.

Agenda

  • Group/parallel technical events and workshops
  • Cultural showcase & networking sessions
